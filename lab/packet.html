2 · What the manuals already say

The VFD: the output side is fine, the input side is the question. In delta the spindle's full-load current is 27.4 A continuous, 31.2 A at peak duty — a 50 A drive covers that with room. But on single-phase the drive pulls all of that power through two input legs instead of three: about 45–55 A continuous, 55–70 A at peak. An 11 kW drive's front end is built for about 40–50 A a leg, and its capacitors for three-phase ripple. Full power needs one size up, and a 70–80 A feed (sheet B).

  • The old Commander SE33400750 is a 380–480 V three-phase, 7.5 kW, 16.5 A drive — a match for the star column. So the machine lived on three-phase 380–480 V.
  • It is in star today unless someone has moved the links: the re-strap is on p.84 of the manual. Photo the board before and after.
  • Locked-rotor current is not listed; from the manual's equivalent circuit it works out near 140 A per leg at 220 V, 200 Hz (p.34). On a VFD it never flows: the drive ramps the frequency and holds current to its own limit.
  • No manual for the exact VEVOR unit turned up in one search — worth getting before it is wired: it must reach 400 Hz, and it should take a braking resistor.
  • VFD settings, from the card — motor voltage 220 V · rated frequency 200 Hz · maximum 400 Hz · motor current 27.4 A.
  • The spindle's other needs — fan on 230 V AC whenever powered, even idle · sensors are PNP, normally open, 10–30 V · thermal switch normally closed, must stop the spindle · never spin unless S1 says "tool locked" · never release the tool while turning.

Sources: refs/HSD Spindle Manual p.33–35 (card, equivalent circuits), p.80–81 (connectors), p.84 (terminal board), p.87–89 (inverter, fan), p.113–115 (sensors) · Commander SE ratings table and VEVOR buyer reports via web search, listed in the README's findings.

The drives check out on paper. CS3E is a drive, not a motor — each needs a stepper. Ruled likely 2026-11-12, on Nathan's recommendation: new NEMA 34 Leadshine closed-loop motors, matched to the drives. NEMA 34 motors pair with the CS3E-D1008 (30–100 V DC, 8 A), not the D507 (20–50 V DC, 7 A). LinuxCNC has a Leadshine driver that lists the D1008; one forum user homed a NEMA 34 with it. Only the dual-axis 2CS3E-D507 is marked tested, which is why one drive gets proven on the bench before four get a cabinet.

3 · The survey sheet — print from here

The rule: photo first, label second, unplug last. Wide shot, then close-up, then the nameplate square-on and readable. Number wires with tape flags on both sides of every connector before it comes apart. Write answers on this sheet; photos carry the rest.

A · The state of things

  • Does the machine still power up? yes / no / unknown
  • Wide photos: whole machine, each side, the cabinet door open, the gantry, the spindle carriage
  • Any AXYZ serial plate on the frame — photo

B · Shop power (decides the VFD's size)

  • The breaker that will feed the machine: A · can it grow to 70–80 A?
  • Voltage, measured or from the panel: V
  • Wire run from panel to machine: gauge · length
  • Photo: the panel label, the breaker, the machine's old plug or disconnect
  • Compressor: pressure it holds psi · dryer or filter fitted?

C · Spindle

  • Nameplate, square-on, every character readable — model, code starting H1…, volts, hertz, amps, rpm
  • Open the small cover on the spindle's electrical box (two screws): photo the terminal board and its links as found, before any re-strap
  • Tool holder plate: ISO 30 or HSK?
  • Both connectors, mated and unmated; count the pins in use
  • Manual release button on the spindle? yes / no
  • Air lines into the spindle: how many · tube size
  • Fan at the rear: present? spins freely?
  • Turn the shaft by hand: smooth / gritty / rumble
  • Tool holders and collets on hand: count

D · Old VFD (free answers, if it still lights up)

  • Nameplate photo
  • If it powers: step through every keypad parameter and write number → value on the back of this sheet. Max speed, motor volts, motor amps and rated frequency are the spindle's settings as AXYZ commissioned it
  • Photo every control wire on its terminals before removal
  • Braking resistor fitted? Photo its label
